# Rule: No Platform Comparisons (Self-Contained Content Invariant)

This rule establishes the inviolable standard for tone, copy, documentation, and product specifications across **Bona Loko**.

## Core Invariant
> **Comparing to others is NOT one of the platform purposes.**
> Bona Loko stands entirely on its own merits, foundational values, and intentional design. The platform never validates, defines, or elevates itself by comparing against, contrasting with, or diminishing other apps, traditional trackers, or external products.

---

## Inviolable Directives

1. **Zero External App Comparisons**:
   - Never use phrasing such as *"Unlike other apps..."*, *"Traditional apps reduce..."*, *"Unlike simplistic satisfaction surveys..."*, *"Most productivity tools..."*, or *"Conventional habit checklists..."*.
   - Do not create visual comparison tables, matrices, or dichotomies contrasting "Traditional / Other Tools ❌" with "Bona Loko 🟢".

2. **Affirmative & Autonomous Description**:
   - Always describe Bona Loko's models, features, workflows, and philosophy affirmatively and self-containedly.
   - Articulate *what Bona Loko is, how its features function, and how they serve human flourishing*, without referencing or criticizing external tools.

3. **Compassionate, Non-Antagonistic Tone**:
   - Consistent with our 7 Core Values ([FOUNDATION.md](../../FOUNDATION.md)), communication must foster peace of mind, self-reflection, and clarity—not cynical industry critiques, dark-pattern accusations, or competitive hostility.

---

## Scope of Application

This standard is universal and non-negotiable across all repository artifacts:

- **Product Direction & Vision**: [`direction/`](../../direction/)
- **Living Specifications**: [`specs/`](../../specs/)
