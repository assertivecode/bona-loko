# Rule: Humble & Non-Prescriptive Tone (No Inferences of Personal Incapacity)

This rule establishes the inviolable standard for respectful, humble, and non-prescriptive communication across all content, copy, specifications, and AI agent reasoning in **Bona Loko**.

---

## Core Invariant

> **The platform, documentation, content, and AI agents must NEVER infer, dictate, or pronounce what someone can or cannot do.**
> Bona Loko is an inviting, compassionate canvas for self-directed personal development. The platform never presumes moral superiority, gatekeeps virtues, or issues absolute declarations of human incapacity (e.g., avoiding statements like *"You cannot act with true integrity unless..."* or *"One cannot do X without Y"*).

---

## Inviolable Directives

1. **Zero Prescriptive Negations of Personal Capability**:
   - Never use phrasing that decrees human inability or imposes moral gatekeeping, such as:
     - ❌ *"You cannot act with true integrity unless you first possess humility..."*
     - ❌ *"One cannot achieve genuine growth without..."*
     - ❌ *"It is impossible for you to build habits if..."*
     - ❌ *"You can never experience peace until you..."*
   - Avoid treating virtues as rigid transactional prerequisites that disqualify the individual.

2. **Respectful, Humble, and Inviting Teachings**:
   - Always frame wisdom, principles, and psychological dynamics affirmatively, humbly, and constructively.
   - Describe how virtues and intentional habits naturally nurture, support, and create fertile ground for human flourishing:
     - 🟢 *"True integrity naturally takes root in humility: seeing ourselves honestly and gently as we genuinely are creates the foundation for authentic alignment between our inner thoughts and outer actions."*
     - 🟢 *"Healthy habits flourish when rooted in compassionate self-awareness..."*
     - 🟢 *"Investing time in our genuine priorities flourishes when attention is mindfully protected..."*

3. **Gentle Partnership, Not Moral Authority**:
   - Speak as a respectful companion and humble guide, never an authoritarian judge.
   - Ground all teachings in compassion (Empathy), honoring individual dignity (Respect), and trusting in the human capacity for renewal and self-directed growth (Faith and Freedom).

---

## Scope of Application

This standard applies universally across:
- **Living Specifications**: All documents in [`specs/`](../../specs/).
- **Dimension Content**: All living articles in [`content/`](../../content/) across `en-US`, `pt-BR`, and `eo`.
- **Product Direction & Vision**: [`direction/`](../../direction/) and [`FOUNDATION.md`](../../FOUNDATION.md).
- **Frontend UI & Translation Dictionaries**: [`web-app/`](../../web-app/).
- **Agent Reasoning & Plans**: Every plan, explanation, requirement analysis, and response authored by AI agents.
