# Rule: Growth-Focused Vocabulary (No Default Religious/Sanctity Language)

This rule establishes the standard for **grounded, growth-focused vocabulary** across all general personal-development content, copy, specifications, and AI agent reasoning in **Bona Loko**.

---

## Core Principle

> **General personal-growth content should use language that frames becoming a better person as an intentional, daily human practice — step by step, day by day.**
> Religious and sanctity vocabulary (e.g., *sanctuary, sacred, holy, sabbath, blessing, prayer, sanctity, hallowed, consecrated, ordained, worship, temple, divine*) should **not** be used as the default vocabulary for personal-growth topics. These words are reserved for contexts where the subject matter genuinely requires religious or spiritual framing (e.g., an article about a faith tradition or a specifically religious life dimension).

---

## Inviolable Directives

1. **Default-Avoid Religious/Sanctity Vocabulary in General Content**:
   - When writing about personal growth, habit-building, life balance, or self-improvement, prefer grounded, humanistic alternatives:
     - ❌ *"The sacred boundary of rest"* → 🟢 *"The protected boundary of rest"*
     - ❌ *"a financial sanctuary"* → 🟢 *"a financial safety net"*
     - ❌ *"sabbath rest"* → 🟢 *"renewal rest"*
     - ❌ *"daily blessings"* → 🟢 *"daily gifts"*
     - ❌ *"single-task devotion"* → 🟢 *"single-task dedication"*
     - ❌ *"spiritual peace"* → 🟢 *"inner peace"*

2. **Preferred Alternative Vocabulary**:
   | Default-Avoid | Preferred Alternatives |
   |---|---|
   | sanctuary | haven, retreat, refuge, safe space, foundation |
   | sacred | cherished, deeply valued, essential, protected |
   | holy | deeply meaningful, revered, treasured |
   | sabbath | renewal (day/rest), rest day |
   | blessing(s) | gift(s), enrichment, good fortune, positive impact |
   | sanctity | trust, integrity, importance |
   | devotion | dedication, commitment, wholehearted focus |
   | spiritual | inner, personal, reflective |
   | prayer | quiet reflection, contemplation, stillness |
   | divine | profound, extraordinary, deeply meaningful |

3. **Context-Appropriate Exceptions**:
   - Religious/sanctity vocabulary **is acceptable** when the content subject genuinely requires it:
     - ✅ An article or dimension explicitly about faith, religion, or spiritual practice.
     - ✅ A user-requested piece about a specific religious tradition.
     - ✅ Quoting or referencing religious texts, teachings, or cultural practices.
   - When in doubt, ask: *"Does this word serve the subject matter, or is it decorative religiosity?"* If decorative, replace it.

4. **Day-by-Day, Step-by-Step Growth Framing**:
   - Content should emphasize **practical, incremental human effort** rather than mystical transformation.
   - Language should feel approachable, grounded, and empowering — like a thoughtful companion walking alongside someone on their growth journey.

---
