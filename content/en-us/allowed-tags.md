## 🏷️ Tag Usage Rules

1. **Format**: All tags must be lowercased and hyphen-separated (kebab-case).
2. **Cardinality**: Each article should select between **3 and 6** relevant tags from this approved list.
3. **Core Alignment**: Every dimension article must include its canonical dimension tag (e.g. `focus-mastery`, `physical-health`) plus 2 to 5 specific topic or habit tags.
